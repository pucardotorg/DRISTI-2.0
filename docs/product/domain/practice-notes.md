## `ke-scrutiny-officer-2026-07` — kerala

- **Heard from:** Mehul (PUCAR Team); recorded as secondhand
- **Themes:** pre-cognizance-scrutiny, gatekeeping-rent-seeking
- **Tags:** corruption, informal-practice, gatekeeping, delay
- **Summary excerpt:** In Kerala a scrutiny officer reviews litigant and advocate submissions and marks defects before the file is sent for taking cognizance. Reportedly the role is informal and not in the rules, and the officer often does not mark defects properly, holding files so the advocate approaches him and may make a payment to move the file forward.
