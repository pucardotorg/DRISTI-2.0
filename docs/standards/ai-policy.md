# AI policy compliance

Snapshot: 2026-07-30. Source: [domain model site](https://dristidomain.netlify.app/)
(Standards → AI policy; policy manifest under `data/policy/`).

## Instrument

Supreme Court of India draft *Regulations for Use of AI in Courts, 2026* — published for
comment June 2026, **not yet in force**. National policy governing how courts run
generally, not case-type-specific.

## Why it appears in the domain model

The domain layer includes a clause-by-clause read of what the draft would require of a
**court and its vendor** — e.g. register, audit log, incident disclosure,
human-in-the-loop. Directly relevant because DRISTI is a vendor product a court would
deploy.

## What this repo keeps

Framing and pointer only. Do not treat draft clauses as binding law; verify against the
current published instrument and the domain site's policy data before relying on them for
design or compliance claims.
