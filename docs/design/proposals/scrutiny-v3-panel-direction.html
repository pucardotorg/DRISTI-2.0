Suggestions not drawn here
Traversal, so eight feels like threeAccepting should land you on the next open defect — card opens, form scrolls, focus moves. With ⌘↵ to accept and esc to cancel an Ignore, a clean return is cleared without the mouse. This is the biggest single win left; a queue you work through beats a list you pick from.
Undo stays reachableA resolved row keeps its decision reversible on expand — “Accepted · Undo”. Cheap to build, and it removes the fear that makes people re-read every correction twice.
On a phone, the panel is the screenThe record collapses to a link per defect (“see it on the form”). Do not try to keep three panes; the workbench is the only pane that matters, and it already works alone.
Considered and rejected · bulk accept“Accept all” would clear a return in one click and destroy the thing that makes this design honest — every changed value in a correction round has a named author. Speed comes from traversal, not from batching away the decision.
