What this changes
Removed entirelyThe inset under the field · the “Scrutiny flagged” tag · amber field fills · the three-tier accordion · the always-visible reason textarea · the per-row warning badges · the second place a defect speaks.
Kept, movedThe officer's note, voice remark and annotated scan — now behind one disclosure. Replace-this-document keeps the same shape: the card's primary action becomes Replace, its Ignore stays a reason.
Still trueResolution is derived from the filing, never self-certified · nothing unflagged can be edited · Ignore with nothing written leaves the defect open and the gate shut · one teal action per view.
Open for youDoes the centre form still earn a third of the width if it is read-only? A narrower record — or a peek-on-hover — would give the panel room to breathe further.
